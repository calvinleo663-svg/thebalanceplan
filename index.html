The thesis

Businesses don't switch accountants on a random Tuesday. They switch when something just changed: new money, a new site, a new country.

So I'd call on the change, not the company. A fresh round means a board that wants a monthly pack. A second site means two tills and three delivery apps. A US launch means a second currency. Each one is the moment the old accountant starts to feel slow, and Balance closes in days.

03 · On the phone

The 30-second opener

Written for Bar Douro's owner, the day after the Soho opening. Press start and read along at talking speed.

Cold call · hospitality
0:00

"Hi, it's Calvin from Balance. You weren't expecting me, so I'll be quick. Can I have 30 seconds and then you tell me if it's worth another two?"

0:07

"Congrats on Greek Street. That's three sites now. Most owners I speak to say the third is when the books stop keeping up: three tills, the delivery apps, and month-end turns into a scramble."

0:17

"We're an accounting firm that runs AI agents. They reconcile your takings every day, and a real accountant signs off. So you know each site's margin this week, not next month."

0:26

"Is that a headache for you yet, or is your current setup coping fine?"

0:00

If they say "we've got an accountant"

"Good, most people we help do. Quick question: when did you last see last month's margin for each site? If it's within a week, I'll leave you alone. If not, that's the gap we close. Fifteen minutes with our team, no obligation."

What I'm qualifying for

  • Need: is month-end late, or are margins a guess?
  • Fit: on Xero, QuickBooks or Business Central, or happy to be
  • Decision: owner, founder or whoever signs the accountant's invoice
  • Timing: is a year-end, VAT quarter or board meeting coming up?
04 · How I'd run week one

The ad says you're allergic to vanity metrics. So the scorecard is meetings booked and meetings that turn into a second call. Dials and opens are just how I find out what works.

Wed

Thu
